the short version

Most maps show cyclists roads. Zyflow shows them where to stop: stays, coffee stops and restaurants that welcome bikes, plus chargers, water and repair. Road closures and bike theft by area are on the map too, so riders can explore without worry.

Zyflow is built by HERKO d.o.o. in Slovenia and launched publicly on iOS and Android in 2026.
