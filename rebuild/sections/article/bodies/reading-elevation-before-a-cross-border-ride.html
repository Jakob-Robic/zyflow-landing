reading elevation before a cross-border ride

How to read the climbs on a ride like Ljubljana to Trieste, and where to plan your stops.

A ride from Ljubljana to Trieste sounds like it should be downhill all the way. You start inland and finish at the sea. But the Karst plateau sits in between, and the shape of the climb before the coast decides how the whole day feels. Reading the elevation before you leave is what turns a hard day into a good one.

look at the shape, not just the total

Total climbing tells you how hard a ride is. The elevation profile tells you when it's hard. A long steady rise in the first half feels very different from a steep ramp late in the day, even if the numbers are the same. In Zyflow, the elevation profile is colour-coded by gradient and the main climbs are marked, so you can see at a glance where the work is.

find the long climb

  • The steady rise. On a route like this, most of the effort comes as you climb gradually onto the Karst. It's rarely steep, but it's long, and it's where the battery works hardest
  • The high ground. Once you're up, the plateau rolls. This is the place to stop, eat and recharge before the coast
  • The drop to the sea. The descent towards Trieste is the reward. Take it easy, the roads get busier near the city
"Total climbing tells you how hard a ride is. The elevation profile tells you when it's hard."

plan your stops around the climbs

The best place for a break is usually just before or just after the biggest effort. Turn on Coffee, Restaurants and Utility in Explore and look at what sits along the route near the top of the climb. A lunch stop with a charger there means you start the descent with a full battery and a full stomach.

crossing a border

Zyflow is live in 8 countries, so places and routes don't stop at the border. Before you go, check Road conditions along your planned route for closures or construction on either side, and remember that opening hours and holidays can differ from one country to the next.

ride it once, read it better next time

After your first cross-border ride, the elevation profile will start to look different. You'll know what a long rise feels like on your bike, and you'll plan the next one faster. Start with a route like Ljubljana to Trieste, plan the stops around the climb, and enjoy the sea at the end.
