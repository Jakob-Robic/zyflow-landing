zyflow is live on ios and android

Free on both stores and live in 8 countries. Here's what to try on your next ride.

Zyflow is now free on the App Store and Google Play, and live in 8 countries. We're just getting started, and we built it around one simple idea: explore bike-friendly places, and ride there feeling safe. Here's what to try first.

explore the places around you

Open Explore and you'll see the map in layers. Stay, Coffee and Restaurants show you where to stop, sleep and eat. Utility brings in the practical things: charging, water, bike shops, repair and bike parking. Turn on only what you need, and the map stays clean.

filter for bike-friendly

Switch on Bike-friendly only and the map narrows to places with real facilities for riders, like bike parking, secure storage, a pump or a charger. It's the quickest way to find a café you can actually ride to and a stay that has room for your bike.

know what's on the road

Before you head out, check Road conditions nearby on Home. Zyflow shows closures, construction and events around you and along a planned route, so you're not surprised halfway through. The Safety layer on the map shows bike theft by area, which helps when you're deciding where to leave the bike.

plan a ride when you want one

Found a place you want to reach? Plan a route there. On an e-bike, the battery plan takes the climbs, your range and the weather into account and shows chargers along the way. Planning works offline-ready too, so a patchy signal in the hills doesn't leave you guessing.

help us grow

This is the first version, and the map gets better with every place added. If you run a café, restaurant, stay or charger, listing it is free while we launch. If you spot something missing or wrong, tell us. We read everything.
