Why
Zyflow?

battery-aware routing

Traditional apps ignore your battery. Zyflow plans around your range, elevation, and how hard the climbs hit, so the ride doesn’t end early.

live charging stops

Chargers are placed where you need them along the route, with detour time and plug details, mapped across thousands of locations and growing by country.

weather along the route

Short, timely alerts for rain, wind, and temperature changes on your path, not just a forecast for where you started.
