You can ask HERKO d.o.o. to delete your Zyflow account and the personal data we hold about you. This page describes the current request process.

## How to request deletion

1. Send an email from the address on your Zyflow account to [support@zyflow.eu](mailto:support@zyflow.eu) or [legal@zyflow.eu](mailto:legal@zyflow.eu).
2. Use a clear subject such as **Account deletion request**.
3. Tell us the email address of the account you want deleted. We may ask you to confirm that you control that address.

We will confirm when the request is complete. You also have the right under GDPR to request confirmation that your personal data has been erased.

## What we delete

When we delete your account we remove, or anonymise, personal data linked to that account, including:

- Account and identity data (email address and optional profile details)
- Saved routes and bookmarked locations
- Push notification tokens
- Strava connection tokens, if you connected Strava
- Other personal data described in our [Privacy Policy](/legal-pages/privacy-policy), except where a legal retention duty applies

## What we may keep

Some information is not deleted in identifiable form, or is kept for a limited period where the law requires it:

- Community contributions (for example Point of Interest reports) may be **anonymised** and kept as part of the shared community dataset
- Location submission reports may be retained until resolved, then anonymised
- Legal and accounting records may be kept for **10 years** where Slovenian law requires it

[VERIFY: retention periods still match the Privacy Policy]

## After deletion

Once deletion is complete you will not be able to sign in to that account. If you want to use Zyflow again you will need to create a new account.
