# K16 — Tržič – Jezersko

A demanding forest link from Tržič toward Jezersko — quiet, steep in places, rewarded with solitude and mountain views.

Route: Tržič–Dovžanova soteska–Jelendol–Medvodje–Bukov hrib–Pečovnik–Jezersko
