# Blejčanka

Probably one of the most beautiful links in the Kranjska roža network. From central Kranj head toward Jamnik; after a few kilometres of climbing, the Karavanke open up. The route continues toward Kropa’s ironworking heritage, then Bled with its lake and castle, and Radovljica’s old town before returning toward Kranj.

**Distance:** 79 km  
**Climb:** 944 m

## Highlights

- Kropa
- Bled & Bled Castle
- Radovljica
