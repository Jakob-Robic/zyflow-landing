# Jezerjanka

Via Maistrov trg past Brdo park, drop into the start of the Kokra canyon and follow it to Preddvor. Past Lake Črnava the road climbs the narrow valley to Jezersko — Slovenia’s first mountaineering village — with Planšarsko jezero and high alpine views before the return toward Kranj.

**Distance:** 76 km  
**Climb:** 603 m

## Highlights

- Learning trail along the Kokra
- Preddvor
- Jezersko
- Planšarsko jezero

*Source: [Visit Kranj](https://www.visitkranj.com/aktivni-oddih/kranjska-kolesarska-roza/jezerjanka/)*
