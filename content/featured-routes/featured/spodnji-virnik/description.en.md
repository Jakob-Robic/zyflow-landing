# Spodnji Virnik – Kehnjak tufa quarry

Zgornje Jezersko–Spodnji Kraj–kamnolom lehnjaka–Sp. Virnik–Zgornje Jezersko

A shorter educational loop to the tufa-forming spring and Spodnji Virnik organic farm. One climb can be walked if needed.

**Distance:** 9.4 km  
**Climb:** 304 m  
**Time:** 1:30 h

*Source: [jezersko.info](https://www.jezersko.info/jezerska-kolesarska-pot.html)*
