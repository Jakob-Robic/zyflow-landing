# Ljubljančanka

Plečnik’s Ljubljana — among the most important Gesamtkunstwerke of the 20th century — is a global urban landmark.

From central Kranj cross Delavski most and ride the right bank of the Sava to Zbilje, then toward Goričane and on via Sora to Topol nad Katarino. The climb is rewarded with views toward the Ljubljana basin; the descent leads into the capital. After exploring the city, return toward Kranj past Smlednik Castle.

**Distance:** 70 km  
**Climb:** 460 m

## Highlights

- Ljubljana Castle
- Tivoli Park
- Smlednik Castle
