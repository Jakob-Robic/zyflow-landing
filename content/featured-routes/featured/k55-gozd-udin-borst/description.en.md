# K55 — Gozd – Udin boršt

A varied foothill loop linking Gozd under Kriška gora with Udin boršt — mountain views without committing to high alpine terrain.

Route: Tržič–Križe–Poljana–Gozd–Zalog–Goriče–Tenetiše–Udin boršt–Kriva jelka–Žiganja vas–Križe–Tržič
