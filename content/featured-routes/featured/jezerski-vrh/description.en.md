# Jezerski vrh – Ankova planina

Zgornje Jezersko–Jezerska slatina–Jezerski vrh–Ankova planina–cerkev sv. Andreja–Šenkova domačija–Planšarsko jezero–Zgornje Jezersko

Climb to the former border crossing at Jezerski vrh and on to Ankova planina for views of Ravenska Kočna and the Kamnik–Savinja Alps. Return via Jezerska slatina, St Andrew’s church and Šenkova farmstead.

**Distance:** 18.2 km  
**Climb:** 457 m  
**Time:** 2:30 h
