# Ločanka

After crossing the Kokra canyon bridge, turn right over Delavski most into Stražišče and on to Sedlo under Jošt above Kranj. A descent into the Selca valley follows, then the ride toward Škofja Loka’s historic centre — one of Slovenia’s best-preserved medieval towns — with Loški grad above. Return options include Crngrob and quieter Gorenjska roads back toward Kranj.

**Distance:** 64 km  
**Climb:** 930 m

## Highlights

- Visoko Manor
- Škofja Loka old town
- Loški Castle
- Crngrob
