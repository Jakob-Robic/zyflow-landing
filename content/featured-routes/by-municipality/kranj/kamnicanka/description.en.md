# Kamničanka

A mostly flat loop that first crosses Slovenia’s only motorway bike bridge near Voglje.

Through Šenčur to Adergas (Church of the Annunciation), past the Češnjevek ponds, Strmol Castle and Grad near Cerklje, then on toward Komenda.

A few more kilometres reach Kamnik’s old town. Via Šmarca the route reaches Arboretum Volčji Potok — worth a stop — then continues through Radomlje and Mengeš to Vodice. From there to Voglje the surface is good gravel through quiet forest. Back to Kranj on flat, well-kept road.

**Distance:** 70 km  
**Climb:** 254 m

## Highlights

- Church of the Annunciation, Adergas
- Strmol Castle
- Kamnik old town
- Arboretum Volčji Potok
