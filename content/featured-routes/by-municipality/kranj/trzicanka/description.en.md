# Tržičanka

From Glavni trg via Maistrov trg past Prešeren’s grove, descend into the Kokra canyon that cuts through old Kranj. Past Brdo park continue under the flanks of Storžič toward Tržič — a town of mountain and adventure routes — then on via Kamen Castle, Begunje and Brezje before looping back.
