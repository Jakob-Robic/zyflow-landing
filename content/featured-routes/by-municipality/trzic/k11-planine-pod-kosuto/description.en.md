# K11 — Planine pod Košuto

One of Gorenjska’s most scenic MTB tours: shady forests and alpine pastures under the Košuta ridge (Pungrat, Šija, Kofce).

Route: Tržič–Jelendol–Medvodje–Košutnik–planina Pungrat–planina Šija–planina Kofce–Ravne–Jelendol–Tržič


*Source: [Visit Tržič](https://visit-trzic.com/sl/poti/kolesarske-poti/k11-planine-pod-kosuto)*
