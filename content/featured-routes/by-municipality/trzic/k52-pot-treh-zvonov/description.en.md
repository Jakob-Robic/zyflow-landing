# K52 — Pot treh zvonov

An easy foothill ride through Udin boršt on the Path of Three Bells — ideal for families and an e-bike warm-up around Tržič.

Route: Križe–Sebenje–čez Udin boršt–Novake–proti Letenicam–Žiganja vas–Sebenje–Križe
