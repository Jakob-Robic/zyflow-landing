## Series breakdown

### K1x — Košuta / high Karawanks (6) — all have GPX
Serious mountain rides: planine under Košuta, Konjščica, Tržič–Jezersko, Košuta traverse.  
**Zyflow fit:** alpine / hard e-MTB showcase — not casual touring.

### K2x — Storžič side (3) — 2/3 GPX
Dom pod Storžičem, Planina Javornik; **K25 Okrog Kriške gore = no GPX**.

### K3x — Dobrča / Ljubelj / Begunjščica (6) — all have GPX
Ljubelj pass, Zelenica, Dobrča loops. Big climbs (Zelenica ~1560 m mentioned).  
**Zyflow fit:** strong “alpine day ride” candidates (esp. **K35 Prelaz Ljubelj**).

### K4x — mid / heritage (3) — all have GPX
Rock’n’roll gravel, **Rožnovenska** toward Brezje, Dobrča hut.  
**Zyflow fit:** **K42 Rožnovenska** good for touring + culture stops.

### K5x — Udin boršt foothills (5) — all have GPX
Easiest block: forest / family / short rides around Udin boršt.  
**Zyflow fit: best first featured picks** for e-bike + battery-aware planning demos.

### KKP Tržič 01–05 — “kolesarske pentlje” (5) — **no GPX**
Curated loops (mountain → Dobrča → Udin boršt → Rožnovenska). Good *editorial* set, but tracks not downloadable from these pages — may need municipality / Outdooractive follow-up.

### Trans Karavanke — **no GPX**
132 km / ~5700 m — multi-day epic (PZS). Skip for first featured batch; partner deep-link later if they provide track.

## Recommended featured shortlist (for Zyflow)

Priority for **e-bike-friendly / marketing**:

1. **K52** — Pot treh zvonov (easy, family, Udin boršt)  
2. **K51** or **K53** — Udin boršt loops  
3. **K42** — Rožnovenska (culture + Brezje)  
4. **K35** — Prelaz Ljubelj (signature pass, closed to cars)  
5. **K11** — Planine pod Košuto (flagship alpine view ride)

Ask municipality for GPX on **KKP 01–05** if those are the “official” marketing loops.

## Overlap with Kranjska roža

Visit Kranj’s **Tržičanka** already touches Tržič / Brezje / Begunje as a road loop from Kranj.  
Tržič’s own catalogue is denser MTB/alpine — complementary, not duplicate.
